**Updated:** 14 August 2026 · **Target launch:** 15 September 2026

### 1. Razorpay test keys ⚠️ THIS IS THE BIG ONE

1. Go to **dashboard.razorpay.com** and sign in (or create an account).
2. Top-right, make sure the toggle says **Test Mode**, not Live.
3. Go to **Settings → API Keys**.
4. Click **Generate Test Key**.
5. Send us both values: the **Key ID** (starts rzp*test*) and the **Key Secret**.

### 2. Start Razorpay live activation — 10 minutes to begin

1. Same dashboard → **Account & Settings → Account Activation**.
2. Fill in the business details and upload what it asks for — typically
   business PAN, GST certificate, bank account proof, and address proof.
3. Submit. You don't need to send us anything yet; just tell us it's submitted.

### 3. Resend account — 5 minutes

1. Go to **resend.com** and create a free account.
2. Go to **API Keys → Create API Key**.
3. Send us the key (starts re\_).

### 5. Channel Partner portal — in or out?

Your requirements document describes a full partner portal: onboarding,
earnings dashboard, referral links, commission payouts. That is **2–3 weeks
of work on its own** and it depends on payments being finished first.

There is currently a partner dashboard page in the site that shows example
data. We have secured it so the public can't reach it.

**Choose one:**

- **A. Drop it for launch** (recommended) — launch on time, build it after.
- **B. Build it** — realistically pushes the launch date.

### 6. Which admin screens must be real on day one?

Some admin screens still show sample data. Three of them (Payments, Packages,
Success Fees) will fill in automatically once Razorpay is connected.

The rest are optional for launch. **We suggest building Users and Partners**
(you'll need those to run the business) and leaving the marketing tools —
email campaigns, WhatsApp tools, segments, analytics — until after launch.

Tell us if you disagree.

### 11. PAN and Aadhaar — how long, and who sees them?

You asked us to collect PAN and Aadhaar on user profiles, and we've built it.
Both are optional for the user, checked for valid format, and visible only to
the person who entered them.

**Two things we still need from you, because they're legal decisions:**

1. **How long do you keep them?** Aadhaar can't be kept indefinitely without a
   reason.
2. **Who in your team is allowed to look at them?**

_Aadhaar is regulated in India. If you don't have a concrete use for it, the
safest option is to stop collecting it — just say the word and we'll remove
the field._
